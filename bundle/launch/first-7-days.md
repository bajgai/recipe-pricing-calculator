# First 7-Day Launch Plan

## Goal
Validate demand before overbuilding.

Success threshold:
- 100 free calculator visitors OR 30 direct conversations
- 10 email/sign-up interests
- 3 paid bundle purchases or setup calls

## Day 1 — Package
- Deploy free calculator.
- Add email capture/waitlist CTA.
- Create Gumroad/Payhip product draft for $29 bundle.

## Day 2 — Manual validation
- Find 30 home bakers/caterers on Instagram/Facebook/local groups.
- Offer free pricing review for 3 products.

## Day 3 — Content demos
- Post 3 short videos:
  1. “Why your cupcakes might be underpriced”
  2. “Ingredient cost is not your real cost”
  3. “How much should you charge for a dozen cupcakes?”

## Day 4 — Free tool SEO
- Publish page targeting “cake pricing calculator”.
- Add examples: cupcakes, custom cake, catering tray.

## Day 5 — Paid bundle
- Release Google Sheets bundle at $29.
- Offer first 10 buyers a free setup review.

## Day 6 — Partnerships
- DM 10 baking coaches, cottage-food creators, or small food-business educators.
- Offer affiliate commission or free copy.

## Day 7 — Decide
Proceed if:
- people submit real products for pricing help, or
- 3+ buy / commit, or
- creators ask to share it.

Pivot if:
- only generic compliments, no real recipes/orders, no willingness to pay.
