# Recipe Pricing Calculator MVP

A free lead-magnet calculator for bakers, cafes, caterers, food trucks, and home food businesses.

## Positioning

**Promise:** Stop underpricing your recipes. Calculate real ingredient, labor, packaging, overhead, fee, and profit-margin costs before quoting customers.

## MVP Features

- Ingredient cost rows
- Batch yield / servings
- Labor cost
- Packaging cost per unit
- Overhead percentage
- Payment/platform fee percentage
- Target profit margin
- Rounded suggested selling price
- Profit per unit and margin
- CSV export
- Copyable quote summary
- Local browser save

## Product Ladder

1. Free web calculator
2. $29–$79 Google Sheets recipe costing bundle
3. $99 custom setup for one bakery/caterer
4. $9–$29/mo micro-SaaS: saved recipes, quote history, ingredient price tracking, margin alerts

## First Launch Test

- Niche: home bakers who sell cakes/cupcakes/cookies
- Free tool title: “Cake Pricing Calculator”
- Offer: “I’ll calculate 3 of your products and show if you’re underpricing.”
- Channels: TikTok/Pinterest demos, Facebook baker groups, Reddit baking/business threads, SEO page.
